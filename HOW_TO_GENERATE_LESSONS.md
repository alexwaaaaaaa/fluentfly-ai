## 📝 EXAMPLE: PROPER A1 LESSON PROMPT

```
Following the FluentFly content guidelines, generate a complete A1 lesson on "Greetings and Introductions".

Include:
- Title: "Hello! Nice to Meet You - First Conversations"

- 15 vocabulary items with full details:
  1. hello - interjection - A greeting when you meet someone - "Hello! How are you?" - /həˈloʊ/
  2. hi - interjection - Informal greeting - "Hi! My name is John." - /haɪ/
  3. goodbye - interjection - What you say when leaving - "Goodbye! See you tomorrow!" - /ɡʊdˈbaɪ/
  4. bye - interjection - Informal goodbye - "Bye! Have a nice day!" - /baɪ/
  5. good morning - phrase - Greeting before 12 PM - "Good morning, teacher!" - /ɡʊd ˈmɔːrnɪŋ/
  6. good afternoon - phrase - Greeting 12 PM to 6 PM - "Good afternoon, everyone!" - /ɡʊd ˌæftərˈnuːn/
  7. good evening - phrase - Greeting after 6 PM - "Good evening, sir!" - /ɡʊd ˈiːvnɪŋ/
  8. good night - phrase - Said before sleeping - "Good night! Sleep well!" - /ɡʊd naɪt/
  9. please - adverb - Polite word for requests - "Please help me." - /pliːz/
  10. thank you - phrase - Showing gratitude - "Thank you very much!" - /θæŋk juː/
  11. thanks - noun - Informal thank you - "Thanks for your help!" - /θæŋks/
  12. sorry - adjective - Apologizing - "Sorry, I'm late." - /ˈsɑːri/
  13. excuse me - phrase - Getting attention politely - "Excuse me, where is the bathroom?" - /ɪkˈskjuːz miː/
  14. name - noun - What someone is called - "My name is Maria." - /neɪm/
  15. nice - adjective - Pleasant, good - "Nice to meet you!" - /naɪs/

- 5 exercises:
  1. Multiple choice: Choosing appropriate greetings for different times of day
  2. Fill in blanks: Completing basic greeting dialogues
  3. Listening: Understanding greeting conversations with different formality levels
  4. Speaking: Practicing introducing yourself with name and basic greeting
  5. Quiz: Matching formal and informal greetings

Make it warm, welcoming, and perfect for absolute beginners. Use only present tense "to be" and simple sentences.
```

## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Batch Generation**: Generate 5-10 lessons at once for efficiency
2. **Use Word Lists**: Reference Oxford 3000, AWL for appropriate vocabulary
3. **Check Frequency**: Use corpus tools to verify word frequency
4. **Avoid Repetition**: Track words across lessons to prevent duplicates
5. **Cultural Sensitivity**: Review for cultural appropriateness
6. **Native Review**: Have native speakers review C1-C2 content
7. **User Testing**: Test lessons with real learners
8. **Iterate**: Improve based on feedback
