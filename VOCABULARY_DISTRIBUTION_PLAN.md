## 📋 VOCABULARY SELECTION CRITERIA

### A1-A2 (Beginner to Elementary)
✅ **Include**:
- Top 1,000 most frequent English words
- Concrete, tangible items
- Basic actions and states
- Survival English
- Everyday situations

❌ **Avoid**:
- Abstract concepts
- Complex phrasal verbs
- Idioms
- Technical terms
- Formal language

### B1-B2 (Intermediate to Upper-Intermediate)
✅ **Include**:
- Top 3,000 most frequent words
- Common phrasal verbs
- Basic idioms
- Work/study vocabulary
- Social interaction terms

❌ **Avoid**:
- Highly specialized terms
- Archaic language
- Very formal/literary words
- Regional slang

### C1-C2 (Advanced to Mastery)
✅ **Include**:
- Academic vocabulary
- Professional terminology
- Literary language
- Subtle distinctions
- Cultural references
- Specialized fields

❌ **Avoid**:
- Only extremely rare words
- Obsolete terms (unless teaching literature)

## 🎯 VOCABULARY TEACHING STRATEGY

### For Each Word, Include:
1. **Word**: The vocabulary item
2. **Part of Speech**: noun, verb, adjective, etc.
3. **Definition**: Clear, level-appropriate explanation
4. **Example Sentence**: Natural, contextual usage
5. **Audio Text**: For pronunciation practice
6. **Pronunciation**: IPA or simplified phonetics
7. **Collocations**: Common word combinations (B1+)
8. **Synonyms/Antonyms**: Related words (A2+)
9. **Word Family**: Related forms (B1+)
10. **Usage Notes**: Formal/informal, British/American (B2+)
