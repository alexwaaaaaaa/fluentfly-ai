## 📞 NEED HELP?

### Common Issues:

**Q: AI generates too few/many words?**
A: Specify exact count in prompt: "Generate EXACTLY 15 vocabulary items"

**Q: Quality not good enough?**
A: Add more examples in your prompt, be more specific

**Q: Taking too long?**
A: Start with 3 lessons/day, increase gradually

**Q: Vocabulary repeating across lessons?**
A: Keep a master list, check before generating

**Q: Exercises too easy/hard?**
A: Adjust difficulty in prompt: "Make exercises challenging but appropriate for [LEVEL]"
