## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. Open the FluentFly app
2. Enter your phone number or email address
3. Tap "Send OTP"
4. Enter the 6-digit code you receive
5. Complete your profile with your name

### First Steps

After signing in, you'll see:
- **Home Screen**: Browse available lessons
- **Progress Tab**: Track your learning journey
- **Profile Tab**: Manage your account

## Home Screen

The home screen is your learning hub where you can:

### Browse Lessons

- **Filter by Level**: Tap level chips (A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2) to filter lessons
- **Search**: Use the search icon to find specific topics
- **Lesson Cards**: Each card shows:
  - Lesson title and skill
  - Difficulty level
  - Your progress (if started)
  - Completion status

### Starting a Lesson

1. Tap any lesson card
2. Review the lesson overview
3. Tap "Start Lesson" to begin
4. Complete exercises in sequence

## Lessons

#### 1. Vocabulary Lessons

#### 2. Listening Exercises
- Listen to native speakers

### Exercise Controls

- **Play Audio**: Tap the speaker icon to hear pronunciation

## Speaking Practice

### Recording Your Voice

1. Tap and hold the microphone button
2. Speak clearly into your device
3. Release when finished
4. Listen to your recording
5. Get instant AI feedback

### Pronunciation Tips

- **Speak Clearly**: Enunciate each word
- **Natural Pace**: Don't rush or speak too slowly
- **Quiet Environment**: Reduce background noise
- **Device Position**: Hold phone 6-12 inches from mouth

### AI Feedback

The AI tutor provides feedback on:
- **Pronunciation**: How accurately you pronounced words
- **Fluency**: How naturally you spoke
- **Grammar**: Correctness of sentence structure
- **Suggestions**: Tips for improvement

## Offline Mode

FluentFly works offline for uninterrupted learning:

### What Works Offline

✅ **Available:**
- Recently accessed lessons
- Cached audio files
- Lesson exercises
- Progress tracking (syncs later)

❌ **Requires Internet:**
- AI chat conversations
- Speech-to-text feedback
- Leaderboard updates
- New lesson downloads

### Using Offline Mode

1. **Prepare**: Open lessons while online to cache them
2. **Offline Indicator**: Yellow banner shows when offline
3. **Continue Learning**: Complete cached lessons
4. **Auto-Sync**: Progress syncs when connection restored

### Tips for Offline Learning

- Download lessons before traveling
- Complete 5 most recent lessons offline
- Progress saves locally and syncs automatically
- Audio files cached for 7 days

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Audio Not Playing

**Solutions:**
1. Check device volume
2. Ensure not in silent mode
3. Close and reopen app
4. Clear app cache
5. Reinstall app if needed

#### Microphone Not Working

**Solutions:**
1. Grant microphone permission in settings
2. Check if other apps can use microphone
3. Restart device
4. Update app to latest version

#### Lessons Not Loading

**Solutions:**
1. Check internet connection
2. Pull down to refresh
3. Clear app cache
4. Try offline mode with cached lessons

#### Progress Not Syncing

**Solutions:**
1. Ensure stable internet connection
2. Force sync from settings
3. Log out and log back in
4. Contact support if issue persists

### Error Messages

#### "No Internet Connection"
- Check WiFi/mobile data
- Try switching networks
- Use offline mode for cached content

#### "Authentication Failed"
- Log out and log back in
- Request new OTP
- Check phone number/email is correct

#### "Lesson Not Available"
- Refresh lesson list
- Check if lesson requires higher level
- Contact support if error persists
